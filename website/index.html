Frequently asked questions

Is 8Sync free?

Yes. The free tier includes two accounts and three AI screenshot imports per month. Premium removes the limits and adds the AI portfolio assistant.

Do I have to connect my broker?

No. 8Sync never asks for broker logins, API keys or open-banking permissions. You add accounts manually or upload a screenshot, and the AI reads the balances and holdings from it.

Which platforms and assets does it cover?

Anything a screenshot can show: brokers like Trading 212 or Interactive Brokers, banks and deposits, P2P-lending platforms, crypto wallets and exchanges, physical gold, and more.

Is my data safe?

8Sync is private by design. A screenshot reveals only what is on the screen, nothing can be traded or withdrawn with it, and you can delete your account and all data at any time from Settings.

Is the AI assistant financial advice?

No. It explains your own portfolio in plain language, as educational information only, and never gives personal investment recommendations (MiFID II).
